## The short version

{{ brand.name }} doesn't use non-essential cookies. There are no advertising cookies, no tracking cookies and no social media pixels on this site. Because there is nothing for you to accept or reject, we don't show a cookie banner.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, tracking pixels and device fingerprinting, do the same job in different ways. Under the ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. No. 336 of 2011), a website must ask your consent before storing or reading information on your device, unless it is strictly necessary to provide a service you have asked for. This page covers all of these technologies, not just cookies.

## What we use

**Strictly necessary cookies.** We don't expect to need any. If we ever do, for example to protect a form from spam, we will list each one here with its name, purpose and how long it lasts.

**Analytics.** Our analytics are cookie-free. They store nothing on your device, and they don't give you an identifier that follows you between visits or across other websites. We see aggregate totals only, such as how many people read a story and which sites sent them. Our Privacy notice explains more.

**Fonts.** We host our own fonts, so your browser doesn't contact a font service when you visit.

## Embedded content

Some stories may include content hosted by others, such as a video or a social media post. We load it only after you click. Until then, you see a placeholder that tells you what will load and who provides it. Once you click, that provider may set its own cookies and will receive your IP address, under its own privacy and cookie policies.

## Links and newsletter sign-up

When you follow a link to another website, that site's own cookie policy applies. If signing up to {{ brand.newsletter_name }}, or confirming your subscription, takes you to our email provider's website, that provider's cookie notice applies there.

## If this ever changes

If we ever want to use non-essential cookies or similar technologies, we will:

- update this page first, listing each one with its purpose, provider and duration;
- ask for your consent before setting anything non-essential;
- make "reject" as easy as "accept", on the same screen and with the same prominence;
- use no pre-ticked boxes, and never treat continued browsing as consent;
- let you change your mind at any time through a link on every page.

That approach follows the Data Protection Commission's guidance on cookies and similar technologies.
