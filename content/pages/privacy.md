This notice explains how {{ brand.name }} handles personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. We collect as little as we can.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is {{ publisher.legal_name }} (company number {{ publisher.company_number }}), {{ publisher.registered_address }}. For any privacy question or request, email {{ publisher.contact_email }}.

## What we collect and why

### Newsletter subscribers

If you sign up to {{ brand.newsletter_name }}, we hold your email address and a record of your consent: when and how you signed up, and any technical details our email provider records as proof of consent. Our lawful basis is your **consent**. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every issue. We do not use tracking pixels or per-reader link tracking in the newsletter.

### People who contact us

If you email us or use a form on the site, we hold your name, email address, your message and anything you send with it. This covers general enquiries, corrections, complaints and story tips. Our lawful basis is our **legitimate interests** in replying to you, correcting our journalism and handling complaints fairly. Where the law requires us to keep or disclose information, our basis is **legal obligation**.

### Website visitors

- **Server logs.** Our hosting records the IP address, date and time, page requested, referring page and browser details for each request. Our lawful basis is our **legitimate interests** in keeping the site secure and working.
- **Analytics.** Our analytics are cookie-free and aggregate. They don't store anything on your device or keep any identifier that could single you out. We see totals only, such as page views and referring sites. Our lawful basis is our **legitimate interests** in understanding which stories are read.
- **No advertising cookies.** At launch we use no advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we never sell your data. See our Cookies page.

## People who appear in our stories

Our stories can include the names, roles and public statements of people in the news, taken from public sources. We process this information for journalistic purposes. Section 43 of the Data Protection Act 2018 exempts processing for journalistic purposes from certain GDPR provisions where complying with them would be incompatible with freedom of expression and information. We still apply our Editorial standards, including on accuracy, fairness and privacy. If you are named in a story and have a concern, use our corrections or complaints routes.

## Who we share data with

We use service providers who process data on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only on our instructions:

- an email newsletter provider;
- website hosting and content delivery providers;
- an analytics provider;
- an email and office software provider;
- an AI provider (currently Anthropic), which processes source material and drafts for our journalism. We do not send readers' personal data to AI models.

You can ask us for the current list of providers at any time. We disclose personal data to anyone else only where the law requires it, such as under a court order.

## International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on a European Commission adequacy decision (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified under it) or the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional safeguards where needed. You can ask us for details.

## How long we keep it

- **Newsletter:** until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days. After that we delete your address, unless you ask us to keep it on a do-not-email list.
- **Complaints and correction requests:** 6 years after the matter is closed, so that we can deal with any legal claim connected with it.
- **General messages and story tips:** 2 years after our last exchange.
- **Server logs:** 30 days, unless we need a specific record longer to investigate a security incident.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data erased;
- restrict how we use it;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- receive data you gave us in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before.

Email {{ publisher.contact_email }} to make a request. We respond within one month, which can be extended by two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you why. We may need to confirm your identity first. There is normally no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at www.dataprotection.ie. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## Other points

- **Automated decisions.** We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.
- **Children.** Our newsletter is not aimed at children, and we don't knowingly sign up anyone under 16.
- **Security.** We limit who can see personal data, use encryption in transit, and choose providers with appropriate security.
- **Changes.** The date at the top shows when this notice last changed. If we make a significant change, we will tell newsletter subscribers by email.
