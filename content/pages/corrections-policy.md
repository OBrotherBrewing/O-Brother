We will sometimes get things wrong. When we do, we fix it in the open, with a dated note that says what changed. We never quietly rewrite a fact and hope nobody noticed.

## How to report an error

Email {{ publisher.corrections_email }}, or use the corrections form where one appears on the site. It helps if you include:

- a link to the story, newsletter issue or post;
- what you think is wrong;
- your evidence, ideally a link to a primary source such as the original data, study or official record.

We need nothing from you except a way to reply. We don't put the details of people who contact us into AI tools.

## Our targets

- **Acknowledge** your report within 24 hours.
- **Decide** within 72 hours whether a change is needed and what kind. If we need longer, for example while we wait for a source to reply, we will tell you why.
- **Unpublish within 1 hour** once a harmful error is confirmed, while we fix it. A harmful error is one that could damage someone's health, safety, money, reputation or legal position, or that seriously misleads readers.

We keep a record of how quickly we respond, so we can see whether we are meeting these targets.

## Types of change

- **Correction.** We got a fact wrong. The note says what was wrong and what is right.
- **Clarification.** What we published was accurate but could mislead, or left out context that matters.
- **Update.** New information has emerged since publication, such as a source revising its figures. This is not an error, but we still say what changed and when.
- **Retraction.** The core of the story can't stand. We withdraw it, and the page stays up with a note explaining what was wrong and why we withdrew it. We don't delete stories without explanation.

## How corrections appear

- Every correction, clarification, update or retraction appears as a **dated note at the foot of the story** (DD/MM/YYYY), saying what changed.
- For a retraction, or a correction that changes the main point of a story or its headline, we also place a notice at the top.
- Every note is listed on our **public corrections page**, newest first.
- The story's evidence file is updated to match.
- We never silently edit facts. We may fix spelling, grammar or a broken link without a note, as long as the meaning doesn't change.

## Newsletter and social media

An email can't be changed once it has been sent. If {{ brand.newsletter_name }} carried an error, we correct it in the next issue, with a link to the corrected story.

On social media, we correct an error in our next post. Where the platform allows, we also edit the original or add a correction beneath it. If an original post is harmful, we remove it and publish a correction in its place, saying that we have done so.

## Watching our sources after publication

A source can change after we publish. We re-check the sources behind every story 6 hours, 1 day, 7 days and 30 days after publication. If a source is corrected, changes its figures or is withdrawn, the story goes back to an editor, who decides whether we need a correction, a clarification, an update or a retraction. If research we reported on is retracted by its journal, we say so on our story.

## Responsibility

Errors are ours, whether a person or an AI tool introduced them. When a mistake comes from our process, we find the check that missed it and fix that check, not just the story.

Decisions on corrections are made by a person, not by an AI tool.

## If you are not satisfied

If you disagree with our decision, or your concern goes beyond a factual error, you can make a complaint to {{ publisher.complaints_email }}. Our Complaints and right of reply page explains what happens next.
