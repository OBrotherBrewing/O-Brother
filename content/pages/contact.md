We read everything that comes in. Using the right address below helps your message reach the right person quickly.

## General enquiries

For anything not covered below, email {{ publisher.contact_email }}.

## Corrections

If you think we've got something wrong, email {{ publisher.corrections_email }}. Please include a link to the story, what you think is wrong and, if you can, a link to a primary source that shows it. We aim to acknowledge corrections within 24 hours. Our Corrections policy explains what happens next.

## Complaints

If you think we have broken our standards, or you want to reply to a story about you or your organisation, email {{ publisher.complaints_email }}. Our Complaints and right of reply page explains the process.

## Story tips

We want to hear about progress, but we need evidence. Email {{ publisher.contact_email }} with "Story tip" in the subject line.

The most useful tips point us to **primary evidence**:

- a dataset or official statistics;
- a published study or report;
- an official record, register or decision.

Tell us what changed, compared with when, and who measured it. A link to the evidence is better than an attachment.

We can't use anecdotes without a record behind them, announcements without data, or social media posts on their own. We can't reply to every tip, and sending one doesn't mean we will cover it. If a tip concerns your own organisation's work, please say so.

## Press and partnership enquiries

For press enquiries, or to discuss sponsorship or partnerships, email {{ publisher.contact_email }} with "Press" or "Partnership" in the subject line. Partnerships never buy editorial coverage. Our Sponsored content policy sets out the rules.

## Please keep sensitive information out of email

Email is not a secure channel. Please don't send us:

- health information about you or anyone else;
- identity documents;
- bank or card details, or passwords;
- information about children.

If you think we need something sensitive to deal with your message, tell us first and we will let you know whether we need it at all. Usually we don't.

We don't use anonymous sources at present, so please don't send confidential or leaked material, or anything that could put you at risk.

## What we do with your message

We use your details only to deal with your message, as set out in our Privacy notice. We don't put the personal details of people who contact us into AI tools.
