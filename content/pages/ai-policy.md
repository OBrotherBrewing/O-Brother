{{ brand.name }} is AI-assisted. AI tools let a small team monitor a wide range of sources and check every claim against the evidence. They also bring risks: invented facts, confident mistakes and wording copied from elsewhere. This page sets out what we use AI for, the limits we put on it, and how we tell you about it.

## The law we work under

Article 50(4) of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) applies from 02/08/2026. It requires anyone who uses AI to generate text published to inform the public on matters of public interest to disclose that the text was artificially generated or manipulated. That obligation does not apply where the content has undergone human review or editorial control and a natural or legal person holds editorial responsibility for its publication.

We go further than the law requires. Even when that exception applies, every story says how it was produced.

## Two modes of publication

### Mode A: Reviewed

This is how every story is published at present. A named editor, {{ publisher.editor_of_record }}, examines the substance of each story before publication: the claims, the evidence behind them, the framing, the headline and the stated limitations. The editor can change, send back or reject any story. {{ publisher.legal_name }} holds editorial responsibility for everything we publish.

Each story carries a short note on how it was made, for example: "Drafted with AI from the sources below, checked claim by claim against them, and reviewed by {{ publisher.editor_of_record }} before publication."

### Mode B: AI-generated, not reviewed

We do not use this mode. If we ever enable it, it will apply only to low-risk stories that pass every automated check. Stories about health, legal or financial matters, crime, children, private individuals, elections, conflict or allegations will always be reviewed by a person first. Stories from a source we haven't worked with before will also be reviewed first.

A Mode B story will carry a clear label at the top, which the reader sees before starting the story: **"AI-generated. Not reviewed by an editor before publication."** An editor will still review it after publication, and our Corrections policy will apply in full. We will update this page, with the date the change takes effect, before any story is published this way.

## What we use AI for

- **Discovery.** Scanning sources and sorting candidate stories.
- **Extraction.** Pulling out each factual claim with the passage that supports it.
- **Drafting.** Writing an original article from verified claims only.
- **Fact-checking assistance.** A second, separate claim-by-claim check of each draft. This supports the editor's review. It does not replace it.
- **Translation.** Only from the approved English version. English is the authoritative text. Every translation says it is a translation and links to the English original.
- **Headlines and social formatting.** Suggesting headlines, captions and short social posts from the approved story. These go through the same checks.

## What we never use AI for

- Inventing quotes, or presenting a paraphrase as a quote.
- Generating realistic images, video or audio of real people or real events.
- AI presenters or voices that pose as humans. Any synthetic voice or presenter will be labelled as synthetic.
- Putting the personal data of people who complain or contact us into prompts.
- Publishing anything that hasn't passed our checks.
- Deciding complaints or corrections. A person does that.

## Images

We prefer charts we build from a story's own data, and typographic cards. Photographs come from licensed sources and are credited. Any AI-generated illustration is labelled as AI-generated in its caption, never depicts a real person, and is never presented as a photograph of a real event.

## Social platforms

On YouTube, TikTok and Meta's platforms, we follow each platform's rules for labelling synthetic or altered media and use the platform's own AI labels where they apply, in addition to our own.

## Records

For every story we log the AI model and version used at each stage, the prompts and instructions it was given, the result of every check, and the editor's decision and when it was made. These records let us explain how any story was produced, investigate errors and answer questions from regulators.

## Our AI provider

We use a commercial large language model provider, currently Anthropic. We send it source material, extracted claims and drafts. We do not send it readers' personal data, including newsletter subscribers' details or messages from people who contact us. If we change provider, we will update this page.
